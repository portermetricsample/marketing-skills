# Strategy — run the 3Cs for the user (don't make them fill a template)

SUMAS step 1. **You (the AI) do this analysis for the user**, out loud, before pulling any
data — you ask or infer the answers, then pick the report's conversion metrics and goals
from them. The user should never have to fill a blank 3Cs template by hand; that's the
whole point of making it a skill.

## The three questions to resolve

Ask the user only what you can't already infer (from the connected accounts, their
website, or the conversation). Keep it to a couple of quick questions, not an interview.

| C | Resolve | Guiding questions |
|---|---|---|
| **Customers** | Who we sell to and how we reach them | *Who is the customer?* (segment, motivations, problems, jobs-to-be-done) · *How do we get customers?* (channels / go-to-market) |
| **Company** | What we do, how we make money, and the market | *What does the company do?* (problem solved, products, business model, pricing, budget) · *What's the market?* (demand potential, size, maturity, how competitive) |
| **Competition** | Who we're up against and our edge | *Who competes?* (direct & indirect) · *How are we different?* (positioning) |

## The outcome — what each analysis tells you to measure

This is the payoff: the 3Cs answers decide the metrics and goals the report will use,
so it measures what matters for *this* business instead of a generic KPI list.

| From this analysis | Set these metrics / goals | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Market size & competition** | Growth vs profitability goals | Startups chase growth & product-market fit; mature companies chase profit. |
| **Industry & segment** | Acquisition & conversion metrics | B2B: MQLs / SQLs in a CRM. E-commerce: orders & sales. |
| **Business model & pricing** | Unit economics: CAC, LTV, Payback, ARPU | B2B: high CAC, long cycles, retention >100%. E-commerce: short cycles, retention ~90%. |

## How to apply it in the report

1. Resolve the 3Cs (ask/infer) — state your read back to the user in 2–3 sentences.
2. From it, lock the **one thing the report optimizes**: e-commerce → ROAS / revenue / AOV;
   lead-gen / B2B → CPA / leads / pipeline. (This is the single decision that shapes every
   later step — see `framework.md`.)
3. Only then move to Metrics. Never present a metric the 3Cs didn't justify.
