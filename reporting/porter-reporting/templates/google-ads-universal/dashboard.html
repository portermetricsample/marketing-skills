Account audit · 8 checks

Health check & what to fix

A read against the porter-analysis Google Ads audit cluster. Each check carries a verdict; the dollar-ranked fixes roll up into Next steps.

Conversion trackingReview

Top value action ("Life App Start") is mis-categorized as Default. Value tracked on Life/Auto only. Down-funnel actions present (good).

Bid strategy alignmentReview

Parallel Life "ROAS test" campaigns. Confirm target ROAS matches the real ~2.6–4.4x and consolidate the tests once a winner emerges.

Value-based biddingReview

Life is eligible for tROAS (value + volume). Health & Dental can't use value bidding until they pass a conversion value — today they're CPA-only.

Spend allocationReview

Life = 73% of spend. Budget-limited campaigns (Life Broad, Health) lose 19–20% impression share to budget while Brand (5.3x ROAS) is tiny. Re-weight.

Campaign settings / geoReview

Nevada spends on many impressions for few conversions — check location/audience targeting. Otherwise geo targeting looks intentional.

Search-term hygieneReview

$1.3K+ on zero-converting terms ("free insurance quote", "insurance near me" broad). Add negatives; tighten broad match.

Quality ScoreBlocked

Can't assess reliably — QS aggregates out of range over multi-day windows (1–10 expected). Use the latest snapshot once the connector normalizes it.

Ad assets / landing CRONot run

Asset-extension presence and landing-page CRO need the asset + scrape pulls (not in this pass). Included in the template; populate next iteration.
