# Format skeleton — Slides (executive presentation)  ⬜ stub

> An **executive presentation**: one idea per slide, highly visual, minimal text. Compresses the
> report's story into a sequence a presenter walks through.

- **Audience:** leadership / decision-makers, quarterly.
- **SUMAS U:** strategic.

## Sketch of the skeleton (to be worked, after executive-report is approved)

| # | Slide | Bound use case(s) | One idea |
|---|-------|-------------------|----------|
| 1 | Headline slide | `synthesis.headline` | The single big takeaway, no subtitle |
| 2 | The number that matters | `funnel-metrics` | One KPI + its delta, big |
| 3 | Why it moved | `segmentation/*` | The one segment that drove it |
| 4 | Risk / opportunity | `account-audit` | The biggest $ at stake |
| 5 | The ask | `rollup.topFixes[0]` | One action |

**Format-specific rule:** one slide = one idea, no paragraphs, no bylines. Highly visual. Same
canonical object as the report, but **brutally compressed** — pick the single strongest point per
section. To be detailed once the report pattern is validated.
