# Worked example — buffer.com

A **human-readable rendering** of [`teardown-buffer.json`](teardown-buffer.json) (the source of truth). The page was fetched live through the Porter Metrics MCP scraper and classified against the framework. Copy only.

> **Declaration:** Homepage · Awareness `solution-aware → product-aware → most-aware` · Sequence `promise-first → feature-led → risk-reversal`

## Level 1 — Strategic levers

- **Positioning.** Claims a *spectrum*: "Your social media **workspace**" (hero) → "management for everyone" (title) → "all-you-need toolkit for small businesses" (metadata). The load-bearing claim is **"connected to every platform and tool you use"** — Buffer as the hub of your stack, not a silo. Competes against single-function schedulers and manual posting.
- **Messaging.** Six recurring claims: completeness · universality · effort-free speed · growth/aspiration · human + transparency · **AI kept optional, not the headline**.
- **Audience.** Genuinely **segmented** — three full blocks, each tuned to the axis that segment cares about, each with its own proof:
  - **Creators** → reach: *"Grow from zero to one to one million"*
  - **Small businesses** → time: *"Level up… without draining your time"*
  - **Agencies** → trust: *"The most trusted tool for freelancers and agencies"*
- **Awareness.** Serves solution-/product-/most-aware; **skips unaware and problem-aware** — opens on the outcome with no problem-education. The biggest strategic choice on the page.
- **Goal.** Free email signup (product-led).

## Level 2 — Structure

The exact sections, in order: **Hero** (workspace + integrations strip + email capture) → **Social-proof bar** (239,262 users + brand logos) → **Core features ×4** (Publish / Create / Community / Insights) → **"…and so much more" ×4** → **Connect your accounts ×11** → **"Whoever you are"** (3 segmented blocks + communities) → **Human support** → **Resources ×5** → **Open-company stats** → **Close**.

- **Sequence:** promise-first hero → feature-led middle → risk-reversal close.
- **Omits:** Problem · Comparison · Pricing · FAQs.
- **Objection map:** answers Fit, Risk, Effort, Trust; **leaves Price, Switching, Need live**.
- **Proof ladder:** anonymous count + brand logos → per-segment named creators → open financials.
- **Copy-component structure:** consistent blocks, but **no bold path** — the story rides on headlines + screenshots, not skimmable copy.

## Level 3 — Copy
- **Concept:** "Everything you need to run social, in one connected place — whoever you are."
- **Angle:** before/after transformation, over archetypes + a listicle of capabilities.
- **Hook:** *"Your social media workspace"* — an identity/outcome hook.
- **CTA:** one repeated verb (*"Get started for free"*), friction removed (*"no credit card, free forever"*).

## Level 4 — Style
Warm, plain, human. Figures: **gradatio** ("zero → one → one million"), **tricolon**, **correctio** ("not just analytics"), **hyperbole**. Triggers: social proof, authority-via-transparency, reciprocity, commitment, concreteness.

## Value read

| Layer | Verdict | Where it leaks |
|---|---|---|
| Clarity | **strong** | instant hero |
| Relevance | **strong** | three real segments |
| Value | **partial** | sells convenience, not a quantified outcome |
| Differentiation | **weak** | never says why Buffer over another scheduler |
| Completeness | **partial** | no pricing, no comparison, no FAQ |
| Conversion | **strong** | one repeated action, friction removed |

**The reading:** a conversion machine for someone who already decided they want a scheduler — sharp audience segmentation, but its defense is zero-price + transparency + human support, not a product-superiority argument. Differentiation is the weakest layer.
